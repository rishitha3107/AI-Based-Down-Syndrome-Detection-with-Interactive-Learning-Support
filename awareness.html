Down Syndrome Awareness & Clinical Guidance

Early awareness, structured intervention, and accessible screening tools can significantly improve developmental outcomes for children with Down Syndrome. This platform combines AI-assisted screening with medical learning support.

Understanding Down Syndrome

Down Syndrome is a genetic condition caused by an extra copy of chromosome 21. This additional genetic material affects development and physical growth.

Types of Down Syndrome

  • Trisomy 21 – 95% of cases
  • Translocation
  • Mosaicism

Developmental & Medical Considerations

  • Congenital heart defects
  • Hearing and vision challenges
  • Thyroid dysfunction
  • Speech delay

Clinical Guidance & Next Steps

  • Consult pediatrician / genetic specialist
  • Speech therapy
  • Occupational therapy
  • Physiotherapy
  • Individualized education planning
